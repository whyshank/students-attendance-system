About The Project

Bridging the gap between technology and education

The Student Attendance System was developed by Shashank, Rehan And Satish to eliminate the inefficiencies of manual roll calls and paper-based tracking. Recognizing the daily challenges faced by educational institutions in maintaining accurate records, this platform was built to provide a seamless, digital-first approach to classroom management.

Our core mission is to empower educators with real-time data, save valuable teaching time, and foster a transparent environment where students can easily monitor their own academic consistency through structured, daily session logs.
